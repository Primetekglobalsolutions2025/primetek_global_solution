## 🎓 Key Concepts Explained

### Monolithic vs Modular
**Monolithic** (current Google Sheets):
- 1 file, 600 lines
- Everything mixed together
- Hard to maintain

**Modular** (redesigned):
- 9 files, ~1,500 lines
- Clear separation
- Easy to maintain

### Caching
**Purpose**: Store data temporarily to avoid repeated database reads
**Implementation**: 5-minute cache in Google Apps Script
**Impact**: 70-80% faster performance

### Status Tracking
**Current**: Jobs just exist, no progression
**Redesigned**: New → Applied → Interview → Offer → Accepted/Rejected
**Benefit**: Know where each application stands

### Priority System
**Current**: All jobs equal
**Redesigned**: High 🔴 / Medium 🟡 / Low 🟢
**Benefit**: Focus on important opportunities first
